# Keep budget capacity separate from repayment progress and preserve plan versions

Budget periods describe the capacity created by income, expenses, and reserve; confirmed payments are shown as actual utilisation and do not rewrite those inputs. A repayment plan is a dated proposal whose linked confirmed payments update progress, while advances, charges, adjustments, unplanned payments, and corrections mark every affected plan for review, including paused plans. Generating a different plan pauses the currently active plan so it remains resumable. Refreshing a plan after review creates a new version, carries confirmed payments already made in the period into its progress, and supersedes only the stale source version. This keeps the ledger authoritative, avoids double-counting payments, and prevents planning decisions from changing silently.
